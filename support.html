Frequently asked questions

How does Oveal work?

You pick a photo, wrap it as a gift, and send it to someone in your contacts. They receive a notification, scratch to reveal the gift, and their phone records their reaction. The reaction video is sent back to you so you can experience the moment, no matter where you are.

What ages is Oveal for?

Oveal is designed for users 13 and older. We verify age at signup. Users under 13 cannot create an account.

How do I block or report another user?

Tap the menu (three dots) on any gift in your inbox to access Block and Report options. Blocked users cannot send you gifts, and reports are reviewed by our team.

How do I delete my account?

You can permanently delete your Oveal account from within the app. Open your profile (tap the profile icon on the home screen), then tap "Delete Account" and follow the prompts. This permanently removes your account, all your sent and received gifts, and all reaction videos. This action cannot be undone.

What data does Oveal collect?

We collect your name, email address, and phone number to create your account. We store the photos and reaction videos you send and receive so they can be delivered. We do not sell your data to third parties. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Why is my reaction video taking a few seconds to load?

Reaction videos are downloaded fully before playback to ensure quality. We're working on faster streaming playback in an upcoming release.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. You'll receive a password reset link from Firebase, our authentication provider. The link expires after a short period, so use it promptly.

I can't sign up — it says "phone number already in use"

Each phone number can only be associated with one Oveal account. If you previously had an account with that number, you may need to recover access to that account or contact support if you no longer have access to it.

Reporting abuse or safety concerns

If you have received content that violates our terms or makes you feel unsafe, please use the in-app Report function on the relevant gift, or email us directly at info.oveal@gmail.com. Reports of content involving minors, threats, or illegal activity are reviewed urgently.
