Oveal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026 · Operator: Matthew Chrisp and William Laird, a sole-trader developer based in New Zealand · Contact: info.oveal@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Oveal (“Oveal”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects your personal information when you use the Oveal mobile application (the “App”). It is written primarily under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and its Information Privacy Principles (IPPs), and includes additional rights and disclosures for users in the European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the United States (including California’s CCPA/CPRA and the federal COPPA rules for children).

Oveal is available internationally. Regardless of where you are, we apply the core protections in this policy to everyone; where a regional law gives you additional rights, those are described in the relevant sections below.

1. Who we are

Oveal is operated by Matthew Chrisp and William Laird, an individual developer based in New Zealand. For the purposes of the Privacy Act 2020 we are the “agency” responsible for your personal information; for the purposes of the GDPR we are the “data controller”. You can contact us about privacy at any time at info.oveal@gmail.com.

2. What Oveal does (so the data makes sense)

Oveal lets you send a “gift” — a photo wrapped in a digital wrapper — to someone by phone number. The recipient opens a link, unwraps the gift by scratching to reveal the photo, and (with their consent) can record a short video reaction that is sent back to you. Understanding this flow makes the data we handle clearer.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Phone number — used as your identity on Oveal and to address gifts to recipients.
  • Username and display name — shown to people you send gifts to or receive gifts from.
  • Gift photos — the images you choose to send.
  • Reaction videos — if you are a recipient and you consent to recording a reaction, the short video you record (see Section 6).

3.2 Information created when you use Oveal

  • Account identifiers — a Firebase authentication user ID, and a public profile record (your username/display name keyed to your phone number) that allows other users to address a gift to you by phone number.
  • Gift and interaction records — metadata about gifts you send or receive (timestamps, status such as “opened”, the wrapper chosen) and the scratch-reveal interaction timing used to play back the reveal.
  • Analytics events — a small set of product-funnel events (account created, gift sent, gift opened, reaction recorded, reaction viewed). These events are designed to contain no personal identifiers — no phone numbers, names, usernames, or photo/video content — only non-identifying flags and internal IDs.

3.3 Information we deliberately do not collect

Your contacts / address book. When you pick a recipient from your contacts, that selection is processed entirely on your device. Your address book, contact names, and contact details are never uploaded to our servers. Only the recipient’s phone number is used — transiently — to look up whether that person already has an Oveal account, and the gift then stores the recipient’s own Oveal profile information, not anything from your contacts. We consider this an important data-minimisation safeguard (consistent with IPP 1 and GDPR data-minimisation principles).

Note for transparency: although your contacts stay on your device, the recipient’s phone number itself is transmitted to our backend provider (Google Firebase) to run that lookup query. That single, transient use is the only contact-derived data that leaves the device.

4. How we use your information (purposes)

  • To create and operate your account and identify you to people you exchange gifts with.
  • To deliver gifts: store and transmit the photo, wrapper, and reaction between sender and recipient.
  • To enable the reaction feature where the recipient consents.
  • To keep Oveal safe — including an on-device content check (Section 7) intended to prevent sharing of explicit imagery.
  • To understand product usage at an aggregate level via the limited analytics events described above.
  • To respond to your support and privacy requests, and to meet legal obligations.

5. Third parties and overseas storage

Oveal uses a small, closed set of service providers. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising networks. The providers are:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) — stores your account data, gift photos, reaction videos and related metadata. Firebase is operated by Google and stores data on servers located outside New Zealand (including in the United States and other jurisdictions where Google operates).
  • Google Firebase Analytics — receives the limited, non-identifying product-funnel events described in Section 3.2. Aggregate analytics data may persist with Google even after individual account deletion (see Section 9).
  • Apple SensitiveContentAnalysis — an on-device Apple framework used for the content safety check. This analysis happens entirely on your device; the image/video being checked is not sent anywhere for this purpose.

Overseas disclosure (NZ IPP 12). Because Firebase stores data outside New Zealand, your personal information is disclosed overseas to Google. We rely on Google’s contractual data-protection commitments and its compliance frameworks. By using Oveal you acknowledge this overseas storage and disclosure. If you are in the EEA/UK, transfers are made under the relevant safeguards described in Google’s data processing terms.

6. The reaction recording — please read this carefully

If you receive a gift, Oveal offers a feature that records a short video of you reacting as you unwrap and reveal the gift, which is then sent to the person who sent it to you.

  • It is consent-based. Recording does not happen silently or automatically. You are asked to consent before a reaction is recorded, and you can decline. If you decline, no reaction video is captured.
  • What is captured: a short front-camera video (and audio, if enabled) recorded at the time you choose to react.
  • Where it goes: the video is uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage and made available to the sender of that specific gift only.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account to remove reactions you have recorded (see Section 9).

If you do not want to be recorded, decline the reaction step. We disclose this feature prominently because we believe collection of camera footage must be fair, expected, and not unreasonably intrusive (NZ IPP 4) and lawful and transparent under the GDPR.

7. Content safety

To reduce the risk of explicit imagery being shared, Oveal runs an on-device check using Apple’s SensitiveContentAnalysis framework on photos and reaction videos before they are uploaded. This check runs locally on your device and does not transmit the content for analysis. This check is a safeguard and is not guaranteed to detect all problematic content. We also provide reporting and blocking tools within the App and respond to reports of objectionable content.

8. Children and young people

Oveal is intended for users aged 13 and over. Oveal applies an age check at sign-up and does not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.

  • United States (COPPA): We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact info.oveal@gmail.com.
  • New Zealand: The Privacy Act 2020 does not set a fixed age threshold, but we apply extra care to the personal information of young people consistent with guidance from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, including the fair-collection principles and the prominent disclosure of the reaction-recording feature.
  • EEA/UK: Where the GDPR applies, processing of a child’s data relies on the applicable age of digital consent in the user’s country; we encourage parental awareness for users who are minors.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child’s use of Oveal, contact us at info.oveal@gmail.com.

9. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from within the App at any time (Profile → Delete Account). Deletion is irreversible and removes:

  • Gifts you have sent, including their stored photos and any associated reaction files;
  • Reaction videos you recorded on gifts you received, and the associated reaction data on those gifts;
  • Your user record and your public profile record;
  • Your authentication account.

Some data may persist after deletion: aggregate, non-identifying analytics already collected by Google Firebase Analytics is not reversed by account deletion, and routine backups or system logs maintained by our infrastructure provider may retain information for a limited period before being overwritten in the ordinary course. We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described, or as required by law.

10. Your rights

10.1 Everyone

You may request access to, or correction of, your personal information, and you can delete your account in-App. Contact info.oveal@gmail.com for access or correction requests. Under the NZ Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to access and correct your personal information (IPP 6 and IPP 7) and to complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

10.2 EEA / UK (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK you also have rights to erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Our lawful bases are: performance of a contract (operating the gifting service you requested), consent (the reaction recording), and legitimate interests (safety, security, and limited aggregate analytics), balanced against your rights.

10.3 California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing”. Oveal does not sell or share your personal information and does not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11. Security

We use the security controls provided by our infrastructure provider, including authenticated access controls so that gift content is restricted to the intended sender and recipient. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Privacy breach notification

If a privacy breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner as required by the Privacy Act 2020, and will comply with any equivalent breach-notification obligations under the GDPR or applicable US law.
